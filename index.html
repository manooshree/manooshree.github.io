Hello! I am a PhD student in Math Education and an MS student in EECS at UC Berkeley. I am advised by Professors Gireeja Ranade and Dor Abrahamson. My research explores how interactive theorem provers and AI can support the teaching and learning of mathematical proof at scale. I lead the development of LeanTutor, a Lean-based AI tutor for undergraduate proofs that provides students with immediate feedback on their proofs and helps make Lean more accessible.

During my PhD, I have worked at Axiom Math as a research intern and at Uplimit as a software engineering intern.

Before graduate school, I worked at Apple in the Health Sensing Technologies group. I earned a B.S. in Bioengineering from UC Berkeley.

Writing

What Math Education Research Can Offer to the AI-Math Conversation
Nov 2026

Recent AI-generated discoveries in mathematics have surfaced fundamental questions about what it means to do mathematics. I believe math education research can offer valuable frameworks and definitions to the AI-math discussion.

I argue two things: (1) productive human-AI interaction in mathematics must preserve mathematicians' agency and engage with existing sociomathematical norms, and (2) Lean code can be used not just to verify a theorem, but to better understand a mathematical argument.
